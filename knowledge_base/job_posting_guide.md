# Job Posting Guide for HR

## Essential fields
A good job posting has: a precise job title, a short company/team intro, responsibilities, required skills, nice-to-have skills, and minimum years of experience.

## Required vs nice-to-have
Keep required skills to 4-8 truly necessary items. Everything else goes to nice-to-have. Too many required skills shrinks the candidate pool and hurts shortlisting quality.

## Experience level
State the minimum years of experience as a number. Junior: 0-2 years, Mid: 2-5 years, Senior: 5+ years. Make sure the title, skills and years agree with each other.

## Writing responsibilities
Use 4-6 bullet points starting with action verbs. Describe outcomes the person will own rather than listing generic duties.

## Fair and effective screening
Screen only on job-relevant criteria: skills, projects and experience. Avoid requirements about age, gender, nationality or photos. Be concrete so the scoring is consistent across candidates.
