# CV Writing Guide

## Structure
A strong CV is 1-2 pages, text-based PDF (no scanned images). Use clear sections: Summary, Skills, Experience, Projects, Education.

## Skills section
List concrete technical skills (languages, frameworks, tools, cloud, databases). Group them by category. Only list skills you can discuss in an interview. Mirror the exact skill names used in the job posting when they are true for you.

## Experience section
For each role write: title, company, dates, and 2-4 bullet points. Each bullet should follow the pattern action verb + what you did + measurable result, for example "Reduced API latency by 35% by adding Redis caching". Show years of experience clearly.

## Projects section
List 2-4 projects most relevant to the target job. For each: what it does, the tech stack used, your role, and the outcome or impact (users, performance, links to GitHub or a demo). Projects are especially important for junior candidates.

## Common mistakes
Keyword stuffing, vague claims without results, listing every tool ever touched, typos, walls of text, missing dates, and irrelevant personal details. Do not include photos, age, or marital status.

## Tailoring to a job
Read the job posting, pick the required skills you really have, and move the most relevant experience and projects to the top. Missing a required skill is acceptable if you show closely related experience.
