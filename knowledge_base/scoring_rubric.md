# CV Scoring Rubric

Every CV is scored only on three criteria. Nothing else (name, gender, age, photo, university prestige, writing style) influences the score.

## Skill match (weight 50%)
How many of the job's required skills the candidate demonstrably has, with extra credit for nice-to-have skills. 90-100: almost all required skills with evidence. 60-89: most required skills. 30-59: some. 0-29: few or none.

## Project relevance (weight 25%)
How relevant, substantial and well-described the candidate's projects are to the job. Projects using the job's tech stack and showing real impact score highest.

## Experience fit (weight 25%)
Years and relevance of work experience compared to the job's minimum requirement and role. Meeting the minimum with relevant roles scores 70+; exceeding it with relevant achievements scores higher; unrelated experience scores low.

## Final score
Final = 0.50 x skill match + 0.25 x project relevance + 0.25 x experience fit (0-100). HR makes the final decision; the score is decision support.
