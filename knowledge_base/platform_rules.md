# Platform Rules

## For candidates
Candidates apply to a job by uploading one PDF CV. Only one CV is allowed per job. Before confirming, a candidate can ask the AI to check the CV, and can replace the PDF and check again any number of times. After pressing Confirm and Submit, the application is locked and cannot be edited or replaced.

## For HR
HR users post jobs, review the AI ranking of applicants who applied to their own jobs, open each candidate summary to see why the score was given, and edit the proposed shortlist by adding or removing candidates or changing the shortlist size. Interview notifications are sent only after HR approves the shortlist.

## Privacy
Applications are visible only to the HR who posted that job. An HR user never sees applications to another HR user's jobs.
